Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers to questions regarding permissions, performance, and integrations.

How does sticky lead assignment work for interns? ▼
When an intern enters an organization or lead into Leadwise, it is automatically assigned to them. Interns only see their assigned organizations, preventing lead overlap. Team Leads and Owners maintain global oversight and can reassign organizations at any time.
What happens to my data if my Supabase tier pauses? ▼
Leadwise includes an automated cloud backup service directly integrated with Google Drive. Every week and month, complete snapshots of your Organizations, Contacts, Activities, and Timeline notes are archived as encrypted multi-sheet Excel workbooks into your personal 5 TB Drive folder.
Where are the serverless functions executed? ▼
All Leadwise serverless lambdas run directly in Mumbai (bom1), collocated with the primary Supabase PostgreSQL database (ap-south-1). This eliminates overseas network roundtrips and achieves sub-300ms latency.
Can we import existing leads from Excel or CSV files? ▼
Yes. Leadwise has a built-in bulk spreadsheet import engine. It auto-maps columns, validates phone numbers, runs duplicate checks against existing records, and allows you to assign incoming batches directly to specific interns or leave them for self-assignment.
